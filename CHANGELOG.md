# LK Nova 1.0 · 2026-10-30

First release under the name LK Nova. The approved 0.900 outline and variation data are unchanged.

- Nine-axis variable display family; 280 encoded characters, 282 glyphs.
- Six static weights from 500 to 950.
- Integrated baseline-aligned red square with a size axis.
- Separate Λ/Ξ, ⇒, ß/ẞ and additional European Latin characters.
- English product presentation with German and Chinese versions, specimen, construction diagrams and downloads.
- Free personal and commercial use under the included permission.
